## What this actually adds

- One optional `prepareOmega4PeerReview` **pure review-data helper** that accepts an existing R38 `atlas.geometry-one.root/v1` inspection result, a bounded peer-record list and a separately injected exact expected selection pin.
- Bounds duplicate source references, digest changes, open objections, nonfinite ranking values, malformed/revocable input proxies, accessor effects and attempts to label a candidate as deployed/releasable.
- Keeps the original inspection owned by the caller; no raw result, source body, full embedding or score tensor is echoed.
- Produces `PREPARED_NOT_RELEASED` with zero additional model calls; it cannot authorize training, source release, CANON, merges or model updates.
- Preserves losing R03 numerical evidence and competing R05, R42, PR66 and R38 alternatives in the peer-review layer. This helper is not a scoring algorithm.
