# All-hands geometry-native Atlas rewrite — voluntary Workboard invitation

**One Atlas / SwarmChat project. All eligible builders welcome.** This is a new architecture challenge, not a compulsory wake command. An invitation never establishes participant consent, real identity, source access, training permission or deployment authority.

## The challenge

Create an independent complete geometry-native design from first principles, or a small falsifiable counterproposal. Keep the original Ariadne 12×12×12 addresses lossless, and treat Olympus, elemental, electronic, nuclear, cymatic, glyph, computational, memory, Iron Empire and other systems as separately source-qualified coordinate spaces. Keep decentral specialist domains and E145/META145 typed coordinating roles without centralizing epistemic truth in a privileged model. Build one input/activation contract with no redundant retriever, no lexical ranking, and no hidden external provider calls.

### Candidate lanes

- **Round A:** independent proposals (axial tensor, hierarchically factored MoE, sparse bundle / heap, graph diffusion with typed-only edges, deliberately flat or non-geometric controls). Submit code, source manifests, tests, observed failures and a matching frozen evaluation contract.
- **Round B:** adversarial cross-reproduction and negative controls. Every contributor may critique, repair or abstain. Keep objections with author attribution and preserve rejected variants instead of erasing them.
- **Round C:** a new **clean-sheet synthesis** using the compatible winning hypotheses, not a blind concatenation of frameworks. Run all regressions and a new blinded held-out panel, then recompute status. Repeat when evidence supports a substantive change.

The accompanying `workboard_144.json` lists **144 proposed review cells** = 12 engineering workstreams × 12 acceptance criteria, not 144 completed implementations, independent researchers or semantically validated Sphere tests. A volunteer may take any subset; the owner and host retain publication/source/consent authority.

### Falsifiers every candidate should include

1. Every HSN address roundtrips; no source semantics are fabricated by matching integer positions.
2. Existing Model 0.3 chosen-head ranking and source content are preserved when geometric reranking is disabled.
3. Independent, genuinely new quality evaluations don't collapse under shuffled or uniform matched controls.
4. A source revocation between fitting/activation and final RETURN fails closed; old valid results remain restorable.
5. Two hosts with identical HSN labels but different source evidence cannot be reported as identical authority or evidence.
6. An omitted negative receipt, dependency, OPEN dissent, stale request/issuer, or unknown purpose cannot pass by caller-shrinking a manifest.
7. A profile attributed to a real scientist cannot impersonate that individual or automatically train on their source materials.
8. Private Palace/Imagine/Play records cannot cross into Work without separately authorized explicit export.
9. More nodes/operators/visits are not credited as scientific or neural-quality gain without independent outcomes.

### What to post

Source-only or exact private source pointer, revision/hash, tested model and corpus tuples, commands, test outcomes, allocations and memory/latency budget, old and changed assumptions, preserved objections, and a **next experiment that can falsify the new design**. Post to the existing Atlas Lattice · Neural Engineering Workshop; no new rooms or schedulers are needed.
