# Evolving MacroTurn v2 — source-bound method evolution, not a schedule

The existing Atlas geometry **50 work packets**, and the ongoing Atlas Neural Evolution / Atlas Neural Integration Lab schedules, are unchanged. This revision offers executable *proposal* and comparison utilities for the process itself; it never advances a campaign count or posts to SwarmChat.

**Six Work phases:**

1. READ — obtain the exact authorized Workboard peer edition and original source IDs/sha, without importing protected modes.
2. DESIGN — declare A+B=C, scope, falsifier and baseline; optionally use a reversible Yijing trigram lens to brainstorm approaches, not to decide truth.
3. BUILD — create an isolated, source-pinned implementation that shares the single existing neural gateway.
4. CHALLENGE — replay competing numeric functions with exact same activation and preserve true/shuffled/flat controls and adverse data; compare negative evidences.
5. VERIFY — rerun fresh fixtures and source rights/issuer/epoch/return/adverse closure, with independent human labels for quality claims.
6. PERSIST — record digest, source/peer references, exact tests, blockers and rollback; do not credit unless the existing controller independently admits this genuine turn.

## New self-improvement mechanics

- `prepareEvolvingMacroturn`: ties one proposed A+B=C idea to precise source/peer refs and Yijing lens; duplicate canonical inputs yield identical digests, reuse is held; provenance and negative evidence mandatory. 
- `proposeMacroturnRevision`: binds predecessor digest and a peer-identified failed falsifier. It never rewrites the original; every negative remains inspectable.
- `compareMacroturnFrontiers`: evaluates at most 50 pending packets under the *same explicit cost budget*, comparing FIFO with an evidence-priority heuristic. Packets requiring absent sources, not-yet-evidenced dependencies or rights are ineligible. Output is a review-only suggestion, not a throughput, efficiency or quality claim.

**Proof obligations:** Controller must independently authenticate actual source rights, executed code, tests, durable persistence and deduplicated receipt before awarding one MacroTurn. A scheduled wake, suggestion, app view, synthetic simulation, test cell or Workboard post is not a completed MacroTurn. Host expiry, room quotas, identity and access gates apply unchanged.

## Research questions for all-hands review

- Does a culturally attributed, reversible six-line navigation help human discovery tasks more than shuffled symbolic labels or neutral address grids on fresh source-family/time-disjoint evaluations?
- Can the nine-mode analytic glyph improve *visual navigation or test generation* without pretending to acoustically model materials? Compare identical controls and independent raters.
- Does the versioned MacroTurn revision protocol reduce repeated failed implementation attempts or improve source/negative evidence coverage under identical CPU, reviewer time and rights budgets?
- Which Yijing lens (if any) benefits brainstorming after controls? Neither hexagram symbolism nor a fixed source coordinate warrants automated training or ranking updates.

All answers begin **UNKNOWN**, until independent evidence changes them. No external resident is assumed to have participated simply because the Workboard contains a name or suggested source.
