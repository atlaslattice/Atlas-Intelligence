# Frontier experiments after the 420-pair synthetic source sweep

The full 1,728-Node lattice supports a lot of possible improvements, but the honest metric to pursue is **verified new knowledge under finite rights and research budgets**, not a count of effects. Some concrete, falsifiable extensions follow.

1. **I18 Mutation Atlas (0/36,288 result changes)**: the inherited operator emits the same static mutation menu for every parent-conditioned packet. That is an *insensitivity finding*, not automatic success. Test mutation effectiveness via a controlled synthetic parser variant, check which mutant is killed and why, then prove no protected source leaks.
2. **I13 Tradeoff Witness (13,465/36,288 changed outputs)**: pairwise cost perturbation often leaves the Pareto frontier invariant. Distinguish robustness from pointless perturbation by constructing meaningful equity/stock boundary cases and independently measuring frontier changes.
3. **I08 Meta-Lens Carousel (16,797/36,288 changed outputs)**: require different domain/epistemic questions to produce an actually different falsifier, not only a changed lens name. Test against equivalent prompts under same budget.
4. **I07 Curiosity Walk (19,491/36,288 changed outputs)**: add curated, reviewed cross-House edges and distinguish true methodological transfer from arbitrarily linking graph nodes.
5. **I09 Semantic Friction (19,491/36,288 changed outputs)**: quantify the *cost of obtaining missing review* instead of turning additional boolean flags into false confidence.
6. **META145 unknown dividend**: pair I02 (gaps), I10 (orphan coverage), I14 (dissent), I20 (allocation) with a *real authorized corpus* and test whether the chosen next review yields more independently useful information than random, degree-ranked or flat-operator controls.
7. **Information compression loss**: use I17 with I05/I03 to check whether a smaller source packet drops dissent or duplicates evidence witnesses; evaluate on human-labeled citation cases, not synthetic hashes alone.
8. **Fair research time**: expose opt-in work allocation at Node, Sphere and House levels, keep the 1/3 Work maximum and avoid treating voluntary Play as a research debt.
9. **Negative-space play**: the same twenty operator names can inspire fictional adventures; those invitations are not automatically classified or exported to Work.
10. **Live release proof**: signed-in two-tenant READ/RETURN test, current issuer-root freshness, current served head, negative replay and rollback. All these gates remain blocked in the local source-only experiment.
