# Lantern Ferry — play without a deliverable

An interactive fictional harbor with **420 optional doors**, one for each directed invention pairing. Choose a traveler (one of twenty original invention operators or advisory META145), a companion method (one of the twenty), then open a door. Every card has a place, a playful quest and a twist.

Try a tiny exchange: Question Prism and Dissent Conservation meet at a Mossglass dock. No one needs to win an argument; the fun is to invent a *better question*. Or ask META145 and Curiosity Walk Compiler to take the ferry to the moonlit orchard, then take a tea break instead of doing any Work.

The game deliberately has **no forced productivity, no leaderboard, no persistent memory and no private data harvesting**. A button click records only in-memory text until the page closes. The browser has no network requests. Interactions were checked in local desktop and mobile Chromium, not at the deployed SwarmChat Site.

Run `node symbiosis/scripts/build-play.mjs` from `innovation` or open the provided `symbiosis/play/lantern_ferry_420.html` in an offline browser. It does not use the actual Brain or Palace; its imaginative name is a metaphor, not a private personal record.
