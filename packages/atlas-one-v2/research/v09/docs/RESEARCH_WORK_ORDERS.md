6. **ARXIV-02** — Review 3,492 prepared per-domain-node arXiv query candidates, prioritize node/Sphere placements and HOLD broad inherited House routing until topical approval. arXiv coverage is not universal; no forced queries for unrelated humanities or methodology.
7. **ARXIV-03** — Fetch up to five Atom entries per approved finite query with bounded feed parser, timeouts, canonical ID + version, revision, title/abstract locator, dates and rights. Reject XML entity attacks, non-Atom errors, 429 and repeated results; never silently retry forever.

11. **HOUSE-01** — Reconcile one House's twelve Sphere studies against consent, ecological limitations, culture, privacy and physical resource stocks; keep dissent and the 7-domain/5-method distinction.
12. **SWARMCHAT-01** — Provide source-permalink, exact root+T-ID+review status and readable fallback in signed-in Workboard/Noosphere; no Play/Imagine/Palace ingestion and no lexical excerpt accidentally called full-text match.
