# 1,728 synthetic strategies: what was and was not optimized

- **Space:** 12 values of family-root diversity preference ×12 values of dissent-preservation preference ×12 values of minimum retrieval score =1,728 numeric policy candidates. Every candidate is associated with an original Ariadne `Hxx.Syy.Nzz` address for the purpose of bookkeeping only. This *does not* say H07 is better at diversity or imply that a node gets a new neural module.
- **Source:** original 2,187,541-byte Ariadne v0.7 owner-private file, verified against its existing SHA256; 1,728 original per-record hashes and 84 domain +60 method Sphere roles preserved. No owner-private corpus text or model weights entered the benchmark.
- **Development:** 144 deterministic synthetic query fixtures with fake ranks and source roots, sometimes two paraphrases of the same origin, one separate contradiction, one source revocation, one superseded revision, one foreign vector and an explicit no-answer group. The synthetic data has distinct family IDs by split.
- **Selection:** all candidates have the same immutable safety checks and maximum number of returned references. A predeclared synthetic objective combines independent root coverage, synthetic relevant-root coverage, contradiction retention and precision, with a hard veto on unauthorized returns and incorrect abstention. Exactly 12 candidates belong to each Sphere, one winner selected; the 12 House rollups are reporting perspectives, not 12 simulated people or owners. One global candidate is locked by development score only.
- **Final:** 144 separately generated holdout queries with distinct root family names. Report baseline and selected results without retuning. This synthetic evaluation is deliberately *not* MICA's silver panel. The generator family is similar across splits, so the apparent gains may be inflated relative to real-world transfer.
- **Audit:** full structural check of all 1,728 records; independent deterministic re-evaluation of 12 development candidates spanning the source graph and full final baseline/winner replay. A full 1,728-row second evaluation was attempted and exceeded the run budget: do not claim it passed.
- **Atomicity limit:** Python simulates READ and RETURN re-checks; only the real publisher can make the return transaction authoritative. TOCTOU race cannot be eliminated by a source-only callback.

The selected parameter configuration is reported as a *conditional best among finite tested synthetic candidates*, never a global optimum or a guaranteed semantic, welfare, scientific or hosted performance upgrade.
