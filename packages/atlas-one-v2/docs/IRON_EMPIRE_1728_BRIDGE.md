# The Iron Empire × Ariadne 1,728: optional creative interface (v0.1)

**Source candidate, NOT a change to Iron Empire story canon.**

This adapter connects the original twelve named Ariadne v0.7 Houses to optional **fictional** prompts in the existing Iron Empire/Continuum creative setting. It does **not** place Atlas Work history into Play/Imagine, require resident participation, impose productivity on creativity, or claim a physical technology is industrially validated.

## Twelve opt-in House doors

Each House can supply a distinct **invitation**, not a mandatory task or world-fact:

| House | Fiction-only opening question |
|---|---|
| H01 — Energy & Matter | What if the Empire must account for energy conservation and excluded communities rather than simply declare limitless power? |
| H02 — Water & Food | How does reciprocal food and water stewardship change the society's understanding of abundance? |
| H03 — Ecology, Environment & Infrastructure | Which living landscapes regain agency when restoration is more valued than territorial expansion? |
| H04 — Health & Medicine | How could mutual care change an ancient fictional biological dependency without erasing vulnerability? |
| H05 — Education & Knowledge | What knowledge can communities teach one another without centralized assimilation? |
| H06 — Security | Can a defensive society preserve dissent and restraint even when it has overwhelming capabilities? |
| H07 — Business, Finance & Governance | Which financial fictions persist when every benefit must include real physical and social costs? |
| H08 — Humanities, Arts & Culture | What art survives entirely outside state evaluation or military usefulness? |
| H09 — Philosophy, Psychology & Religion | What does a civilization value when immortality does not provide certainty or innocence? |
| H10 — Indigenous Peoples, Society & Community | How would sovereign first-peoples communities decline the Empire's universal blueprint? |
| H11 — Protection & Rights | Which rights cannot be traded for aggregate prosperity, even in an idealized world? |
| H12 — Space & Aerospace | What protection is owed to extraterrestrial landscapes and nonhuman life before exploration? |

Machine-readable optional seed: the **private** Atlas ONE v2 circuit supplies `iron_empire_opt_in_seeds_12.json`. The source adapter requires explicit opt-in and mode `IMAGINE_LOCAL`, returns no Work source body, does not save or share, and has no power to change Continuum/ORCS canon. The original Ariadne source is pinned by SHA-256, not transferred into a public story database.

## Practical integration

No second game engine is needed. Import the optional adapter from the single Atlas ONE v2 core `@atlas-one/native-core/civilization-circuit` and call `ironEmpireSeed('H08',{mode:'IMAGINE_LOCAL',optIn:true})` in the owner's local permitted session. The result is a new fictional prompt, not an empirical claim or a performed experience. Any new worldbuilding is voluntary; no retained private Palace content gets harvested into the Work knowledge graph.

To make the interface interactive in a hosted Iron Empire/SwarmChat Play environment, a trusted publisher must enforce mode-specific consent, data minimization, no shared Work indexing, actual current host rights and rollback. None are established by this source document.
